Every gameweek asks the same question: can you find one winner without repeating yourself?

Read the source rules ↗
01

Pick one team

Choose one Premier League team before each gameweek deadline.

02

They must win

A win takes you through. A draw, loss, or no pick knocks you out.

03

No repeats

You cannot select the same team again until you have used all 20 clubs.

04

Last one wins

When only one player remains after a gameweek, they win the round.

Full competition details

Changing picks: picks can be changed up to the current gameweek deadline.

Everyone goes out: the administrator may end the round, roll over the prize, or revive the last players.

Abandoned before play: if abandoned after the deadline, the pick is treated as a win.

Incomplete matches: the score at abandonment is used when a match has started.

End of season: an unfinished round ends without a winner.
